Delete it yourself, in the app

  1. Sign in at astudio.hepius.co.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Under "Privacy and your data", tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm with your password (or with Google if you signed up with Google) and type DELETE.

Deletion happens straight away. Before you delete, you can tap Download my data to keep a copy.

Can't sign in?

Email brush@astudio.hepius.co from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my A Studio account". We may ask a question to confirm it is you, then delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile: name, email, phone, photo, birthday, preferences and push tokens.
  • For artists: your studio page and link, services, hours, deposit payment details, portfolio photos and videos, cover, and any verification files.
  • Your listings in the shop and their photos.
  • Your notifications.

What is kept, and why

  • Past bookings and reviews stay for the other person's records, with your name removed.
  • Messages you sent stay in the other person's conversation, shown as from a deleted account.
  • The activity log keeps a record that the account existed and was deleted, for safety and legal reasons, for up to 24 months.
  • Encrypted backups held by our providers roll off within their normal cycle.

Only want to stop messages?

You don't need to delete your account. In Account you can turn off holiday and birthday messages, booking emails, and alerts on each device.
