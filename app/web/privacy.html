1. Who we are

A Studio (astudio.hepius.co) is a marketplace that connects clients with independent beauty artists in Addis Ababa and hosts a shop for beauty items. A Studio is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: brush@astudio.hepius.co, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

We follow Ethiopia's Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024 and handle data only for clear purposes, with the minimum needed.

2. What we collect

WhoWhat
Everyone with an accountName, email address, phone number, password (stored only by Google Firebase in a protected form; we never see it), language, optional photo, optional birthday (month and day only), your consents and notification choices, and the device tokens needed to send you push alerts if you turn them on.
ArtistsStudio name and link, bio, specialties, areas and places you work, services and prices, working hours, portfolio photos and videos, WhatsApp and Telegram contacts, deposit payment details (Telebirr number or bank account), and for verification a photo of your ID or Fayda card and a short work video.

We do not collect your exact GPS location. Photos you upload are redrawn on your phone first, which removes the location data cameras attach. We do not use advertising trackers.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To run A Studio for you (our agreement with you): your account, studio pages, search, bookings, chat, reviews, the shop, and service messages about your bookings by in-app notice, push and email.
  • With your consent: storing data with our providers outside Ethiopia (section 5), holiday, wedding-season and birthday messages and offers, and push alerts on a device. You can withdraw consent at any time in Account; this does not affect what was done before.
  • To keep people safe (legitimate interest): verifying artists, preventing fraud and abuse, the activity log, handling reports and disputes.
  • To meet legal duties, such as answering lawful requests from authorities.

4. Who can see your data

  • The public sees what an artist publishes on her studio page (name, photos, services, prices, areas, reviews, WhatsApp and Telegram buttons) and what sellers publish in listings. Reviewers are shown by first name.
  • The other side of a booking sees the booking details. An artist sees a client's exact landmark and phone number only once the booking is confirmed. A client sees an artist's deposit payment details only after the artist accepts the request.
  • The people you message see your messages, name and photo.
  • The A Studio team can see account records to run and protect the platform. Verification files can be opened only by the team. We read chat messages only when someone reports a conversation or the law requires it.
  • Service providers who process data for us under their own security terms: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging), our web hosting provider (the website, photos, videos and our mail server for brush@astudio.hepius.co), and, when we send codes or text messages, SMS or Telegram delivery providers.
  • Authorities, when Ethiopian law requires it.

We do not sell or rent personal data.

5. Where your data is stored

Account, booking, chat and listing data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in a European multi-region location. Photos, videos and the website are stored by our web hosting provider, whose servers may be outside Ethiopia. Ethiopian law allows personal data to be stored abroad with your explicit consent, which we ask for when you create an account. If you do not agree, please do not create an account.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account, studio, portfolio and listing data: until you delete them or your account.
  • Bookings and reviews: kept after your account is deleted, with your name removed, because the other person needs the record.
  • Verification files (ID or Fayda photo, work video): deleted within 30 days of our decision. Only the result (verified or not) is kept.
  • Activity log: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Push tokens: removed when you sign out of that device, or when they stop working.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Access to every collection is enforced by database security rules, so people can only read and change what they are allowed to. Uploads require a valid sign-in, are checked by type and size, and are stored with random file names in a folder where nothing can run as code. Verification files sit in a private folder outside the website. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.

8. Your rights

  • Access and portability: download a copy of your data in the app under Account, "Download my data".
  • Correction: edit your details in Account or Studio Desk at any time.
  • Erasure: delete your account in Account, "Delete my account", or see Delete your data.
  • Objection to direct marketing: turn off holiday and birthday messages in Account. Service messages about your bookings still reach you.
  • Withdrawing consent and other requests: write to brush@astudio.hepius.co. We answer within 30 days.
  • Complaints: you may complain to the Ethiopian Communications Authority, which supervises personal data protection.

9. Children

A Studio is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

10. Browser storage

A Studio stores small items in your browser: your language choice and the sign-in session that keeps you logged in. These are needed for the app to work; we do not use advertising cookies.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will show the new version in the app and, where the law requires, ask for your consent again.
