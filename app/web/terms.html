1. What A Studio is

A Studio (astudio.hepius.co, "A Studio", "we", "us") is an online marketplace. It lets independent beauty professionals such as makeup, nails, hair, lash, brow and henna artists ("Artists") publish a studio page, and lets people ("Clients") find them, message them, request bookings and leave reviews. It also hosts The Vanity, where Artists and A Studio list beauty products and equipment for sale.

A Studio is only a medium that connects Clients, Artists, buyers and sellers. We are not a beauty salon, an employer of Artists, an agent of any user, a seller of the items other users list, a payment institution or an escrow service.
